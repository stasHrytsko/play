What this is

A structured product discovery experiment: 30 mechanics, consistent constraints, public behavioral data. It is a personal side project—a place to explore game design with the same discipline I bring to product and delivery work. The full write-up, including why and how, lives on the project case study.

Working principle

Build quickly. Measure honestly. Keep what works.

What’s ready

The hub structure, 30-slot schedule and analytics instrumentation are in place. The first playable build and PostHog project token are the next milestones.

Prototype log

Thirty numbered slots, in schedule order. Each published card includes a cover, its one-sentence rule and a direct Play button.
